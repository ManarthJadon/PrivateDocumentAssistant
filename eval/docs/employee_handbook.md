# Pebblecrest Studio Employee Handbook

Welcome to Pebblecrest Studio. This handbook explains how we work, what you can expect from us, and what we expect from you. If something is unclear, ask your team lead or write to the people team.

## Working hours

Our office is open from 8:00 to 19:00, Monday to Friday. Standard working hours are 9:30 to 17:30, with a one-hour lunch break. Everyone must be available during our core hours, 11:00 to 15:00, because most meetings happen then. Outside core hours you can arrange your day freely, as long as you work 37 hours per week.

## Remote work

You can work from home on Tuesdays and Thursdays. On Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays we expect everyone in the office, because those are our team days. If you need to work remotely on another day, ask your team lead at least two days in advance. Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 days per year, but you must tell the people team first.

## Holidays and leave

Every full-time employee gets 27 days of paid annual leave per year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees get the same amount, adjusted to their hours. You can carry over at most 5 unused days into the next year; carried-over days must be used before the end of March.

Book holidays in the HR portal at least two weeks before you leave. For holidays longer than two weeks, ask at least six weeks in advance.

Sick leave is paid for up to 10 days per year without a doctor's note. From the 11th day, please send a doctor's note to the people team. If you are sick, message your team lead before 10:00 on the first day.

Parents get 20 weeks of paid parental leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks during the child's first two years.

## Your first months

New employees have a probation period of four months. During probation, the notice period is one week for both sides. After probation, the notice period is one month.

On your first day you will meet your onboarding buddy, who helps you during your first six weeks. Your buddy is always from a different team, so you get to know more people.

## Equipment

Every employee gets a laptop and may choose between a 14-inch and a 16-inch model. Laptops are replaced every three years. You also get a one-time home office budget of 450 euros for a chair, desk or monitor. Keep the receipts, because you need them to claim the money.

If your laptop breaks or is stolen, report it to the IT desk within 24 hours. Never store customer files on a USB stick.

## Expenses and training

Work expenses must be submitted within 30 days, with a photo of the receipt. The meal allowance during business trips is 35 euros per day. Trains are preferred over flights for any trip shorter than five hours.

Each employee has a training budget of 1,200 euros per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused training budget does not carry over to the next year.

## Life at the office

Dogs are welcome on Fridays, as long as they are calm and house-trained. The office dog, Biscuit, is a golden retriever who belongs to our founder and visits most days.

Free breakfast is served every Wednesday at 9:00 in the kitchen on the second floor. The quiet room on the third floor is for focused work only: no calls and no meetings.

## Safety

If the fire alarm rings, leave the building by the nearest stairs. Never use the lift. Our assembly point is the blue bench next to the canal, across the street from the main entrance. Wait there until a fire warden tells you it is safe to go back inside.

First aid kits are in the kitchen and next to the reception desk.
